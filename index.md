# **Title:** What's a weblog anyway? #
## Weblog ##
- The term **Blog** is a portmanteau, combining "**Word Wide Web**" (frequently seen at the beginning of URLs as www.) with the word "**Log**" - referring to the ledger or record of events, items, etc..
- The term was coined circa 1999
- Blogs are organized in reverse chronological order, with the newest additions displayed at the top of the webpage and older content following below
